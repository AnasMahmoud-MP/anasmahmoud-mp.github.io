SNAPCHAT + TIKTOK / ABAQ AL-TA'ALLUM

Abaq paid media growth.

Abaq grew from steady always-on activity into a larger Snapchat and TikTok program. The account combined conversion campaigns, UGC, retargeting and regular creative testing.

01Problem

Growth without clear campaign roles

Always-on activity was producing data, but conversion, awareness and scaling decisions needed clearer separation.

02What I changed

Built a repeatable testing system

Separated campaign roles, rotated UGC, retargeted engaged audiences and moved budget toward proven conversion campaigns.

03Result

Scale backed by purchase data

Snapchat reached 3,129 purchases and $192.7k in purchase value, supported by efficient TikTok sales activity.

HOW IT WAS MANAGED

Separate campaign roles, then scale what converts.

Conversion campaigns were judged on purchases and cost per purchase. Traffic, reach and follower campaigns were treated as supporting activity. On TikTok, the sales campaigns delivered 1,282 purchases at a 110.09 SAR cost per purchase, and the strongest fully tracked campaigns received more budget.

09FAQBefore we start

A few practical answers.

The details change from project to project. These are the questions that usually help us decide whether the fit is right.

Which platforms do you manage?

Meta Ads, Google Ads, Snapchat and TikTok. The channel mix is chosen around the objective, market and available creative—not just because a platform is popular.

Which markets do you work in?

Arabic-speaking and international markets. Targeting, creative direction and reporting are adapted to the audience and commercial context of each market.

Is there a minimum ad budget?

There is no single minimum for every project. It depends on the platform, market, offer and expected conversion cost. I assess the budget before we start and will say clearly if it is too limited to produce useful learning.

How does reporting work?

We agree on a reporting rhythm at the start. Reports focus on spend, leads or purchases, efficiency, the tests that ran and the next actions—not a dashboard without context.

Can we work in Arabic and English?

Yes. Communication and reporting can be handled in Arabic or English, depending on what works best for your team.

How does a project start?

We begin with a short call or WhatsApp brief, then review the account and access needed. You receive clear priorities and a plan before any launch or major optimization work begins.
